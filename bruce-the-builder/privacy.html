What we collect

When you fill in the enquiry form on our website, we collect:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • your phone number, if you give us one
  • the type of work you are asking about
  • whatever you write in the message box

If you contact us by phone, email or Facebook instead, we hold whatever you choose to tell us in that conversation. When we come out to quote, we will also hold the address of the property and notes or photographs of the work.

We do not ask for, and would rather you did not send, financial details, health information or anything else sensitive through the website form.

Who else sees it

We are a small business and we do not sell or share your details for anyone else's purposes. Your information does pass through a few services we use to run the business:

  • Our website form provider (name the provider once the form is wired up — e.g. Web3Forms), which takes what you submit and emails it to us.
  • Our email provider, Google, because our email is a Gmail account.
  • Our website host (name the host once it is live), which keeps standard server logs including visitors' IP addresses.
  • Our accountant and HMRC, for invoices and tax records.

Some of these providers operate outside the UK. Where that happens, they are covered by UK adequacy regulations or by standard contractual clauses, which are the safeguards the law requires for transfers abroad.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies. There is no analytics, no advertising pixel and no tracking of any kind, which is why you have not been asked to accept anything. Our host keeps ordinary server logs, as every website host does.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to:

  • Show you what we hold about you, and give you a copy
  • Correct anything that is wrong or incomplete
  • Delete it, where we have no continuing reason to keep it
  • Restrict or object to what we are doing with it
  • Transfer it to you or someone else in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent you previously gave, such as for photographs

Ask using any of the contact details above. It is free, and we will respond within one month. We may need to confirm who you are first. Note that we cannot always delete everything — invoices, for instance, we are legally obliged to keep.
