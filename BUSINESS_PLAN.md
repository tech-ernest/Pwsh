# Business Plan: Digital Laser-Cut Design Files Shop

**Owner profile:** Full-time employed (8–4), 5–10 hrs/week available during setup, <£100/month budget, IT/scripting background, knows laser cutting/engraving and fitness. Wants low-effort, automatable income of £200–2,000/month within 12 months. Light async customer contact only.

---

## 1. The Idea

Sell **digital design files for laser cutters** (SVG / DXF / AI / PDF bundles) on Etsy, with Gumroad/Ko-fi as a secondary channel. Products are things like:

- Layered 3D wall art and mandalas
- Name signs, cake toppers, door hangers
- Parametric boxes, organisers, trays (SVG generated by script)
- Seasonal items (Christmas ornaments, Valentine's, Mother's Day) — these drive huge spikes
- Niche crossover: **fitness-themed cut files** (gym signs, PR boards, medal hangers) — an underserved intersection you know both sides of

### Why this specific business

| Requirement | How this fits |
|---|---|
| Low effort / automated | Digital delivery is 100% automatic. No inventory, no shipping, no fulfilment. Make a file once, sell it forever. |
| <£100/mo budget | Etsy listing fee is $0.20/listing + ~9.5% of each sale in fees. Everything else can run on free tools. |
| 5–10 hrs/week | 1 new design ≈ 1–2 hours. Setup phase is front-loaded; steady state is 2–4 hrs/week. |
| Your skills | You can **test-cut your own designs** (quality moat — most sellers don't), and use scripting to generate parametric variants, batch-export formats, and automate mockups. |
| Light async support only | Customer questions arrive as Etsy messages; a saved-replies FAQ handles 90%. No calls, no personal brand needed. |
| £200–2,000/mo goal | Typical trajectory: £0–50/mo months 1–3, £100–300/mo by month 6 with ~60–100 listings, £300–1,000+/mo by month 12 with seasonal spikes. Top-quartile shops in this niche clear £2k/mo, but plan on the median. |

### Why not the alternatives

- **Selling physical laser-cut items:** shipping, materials, and made-to-order work kill the "low effort" requirement.
- **Fitness content/coaching:** crowded, needs a personal brand and ongoing visibility.
- **Print-on-demand:** thinner margins, more saturated, and you have no edge there.

Fitness isn't discarded — it becomes a **differentiated product line inside the shop** (fitness/gym cut files), not a separate business.

---

## 2. Business Model & Numbers

**Pricing:** £3–6 single designs, £10–25 bundles ("50 Christmas ornament SVGs"). Bundles are where the money is — same automatic delivery, 4× the price.

**Unit economics per £4 sale:** Etsy takes ~£0.55 (6.5% transaction + payment fees + listing fee). ~£3.45 margin, ~86%. Zero marginal cost.

**Volume math for the goal:**
- £200/mo ≈ 55 sales/mo at £4 avg, or ~20 bundle sales
- £1,000/mo ≈ mix of ~150 sales with bundles pulling the average price up

**The single biggest success factor is listing count + SEO.** Etsy is a search engine. Shops with 100+ well-titled listings get discovered; shops with 12 don't. Your automation advantage directly attacks this: scripts that turn 1 design into 5 variants × 4 file formats mean you scale listings faster than competitors who do everything manually in Illustrator.

**Monthly costs (all optional except Etsy fees):**

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Etsy fees | ~9.5% of revenue + $0.20/listing |
| eRank (Etsy SEO research) | Free tier → £5/mo Basic when earning |
| Canva Free (mockups) | £0 |
| Inkscape (design + CLI batch export) | £0 |
| Gumroad (channel #2) | £0 + 10% per sale |
| **Total fixed** | **≈ £0–10/mo** — far under budget |

## 3. Setup Phase (Weeks 1–8, ~8 hrs/week)

### Weeks 1–2: Foundation
1. Open Etsy shop (pick a name that isn't your own — anonymous is fine).
2. Install Inkscape; set up a repo folder structure: `designs/`, `exports/`, `mockups/`, `listings/`.
3. Research with eRank free + Etsy search autocomplete: find 20 keyword phrases with demand and beatable competition ("gym pr board svg", "laser cut medal hanger", "layered mandala svg").
4. Create the first 5 designs. **Test-cut each one** — "shop-tested files" in every listing is a genuine differentiator; the #1 complaint on competitor reviews is files that don't cut properly.

### Weeks 3–4: Automation tooling (your unfair advantage)
Build once, use forever — and this repo is where it lives:
- **Batch export script** (PowerShell + Inkscape CLI): one master SVG → SVG/DXF/PDF/PNG in one command.
- **Parametric generators** (PowerShell or Python writing raw SVG): boxes, trays, and signs with size/text parameters. One script = 20 listings ("box 100mm", "box 150mm", personalised-name templates…).
- **Mockup automation**: overlay design PNGs onto 3–4 photo templates programmatically (ImageMagick) so every listing gets consistent images without manual Canva work.
- **Listing checklist template**: title formula (`[Item] SVG | Laser Cut File | Glowforge, xTool, Lightburn | Instant Download`), 13 tags, description boilerplate with FAQ.

### Weeks 5–8: Volume
- Publish to **30–40 listings**. Mix: evergreen (60%), seasonal (25%), fitness niche (15%).
- Put the same catalogue on Gumroad (takes an hour, it's just uploads).
- Write 5 saved replies for common questions (file formats, machine compatibility, refunds on digital goods, commercial-use licence terms).

---

## 4. Steady State (Month 3+, 2–4 hrs/week)

Weekly routine:
- **1–2 new designs/week** (the only real ongoing work; batch on a Sunday).
- 10 min: answer messages with saved replies.
- 10 min: check eRank stats, renew/retitle listings that get views but no sales.

Seasonal calendar (list 6–8 weeks ahead — this is where the £ spikes come from):
- Sep–Oct: Christmas ornaments/decor (biggest event of the year for this niche)
- Dec–Jan: Valentine's; Jan also = fitness/New Year's resolution line
- Feb–Mar: Easter, Mother's Day
- May–Jun: Father's Day, wedding season

---

## 5. Growth Levers (Months 6–12)

1. **Bundles**: repackage existing catalogue ("Complete Christmas Pack — 60 designs"). Zero new work, highest revenue per sale.
2. **Commercial-use licence tier**: sell a "sell what you cut" licence at 2–3× price — buyers running craft businesses happily pay it.
3. **Email list**: free sample file in exchange for email (Gumroad does this natively); mail the list each seasonal drop.
4. **Pinterest**: laser-craft buyers live there; auto-pin new listings (free scheduler tiers exist). No personal brand required.
5. **If it works**: own storefront (Payhip/Lemon Squeezy, near-zero fees) once you have repeat traffic, cutting Etsy's 9.5% out of your best sellers.

---

## 6. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Etsy niche saturation | Compete on quality (test-cut proof, cut-settings included per material) and the fitness sub-niche where competition is thin. |
| AI-generated file flood lowering prices | Their files often don't cut cleanly (open paths, wrong kerf). "Shop-tested on real machines" messaging + reviews beat them. |
| Etsy suspends/changes rules | Gumroad + email list from day one; never single-platform. |
| Copyright strikes | Only original designs. No brand logos, sports teams, movie quotes, or "inspired by" anything. Non-negotiable. |
| Motivation dip in slow months 1–3 | Expect near-zero sales until ~30 listings. The KPI early on is listing count, not revenue. |
